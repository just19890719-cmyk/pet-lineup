Pet Lineup Support

Pet Lineup is made by Luca studio. Questions or feedback? Email us at kpyluca.studio@gmail.com. Please include your phone model, iOS version and the level you're on, and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.

FAQ

How do I play?
Tap a column to pick up the animal on top, then tap another column to place it on a matching animal. Fill a column with one kind of animal and it clears. Sort them all to win. Stuck? Park an animal in a spare slot.

I'm stuck on a level.
Use Hint to see the next move, Undo to take back a move, or Reveal to uncover hidden animals. Every level is guaranteed solvable, and restarting is always free.

I bought "Remove Ads", but ads came back after I changed phones or reinstalled.
Open Settings in the game and tap "Restore Purchases". Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.

Will I lose my progress?
Progress is saved only on your device and is never uploaded. Deleting the game deletes your progress permanently, and progress doesn't transfer to a new phone automatically.

How do I change the language?
Choose a language in Settings. 14 languages are supported.

How do I get a refund?
Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
