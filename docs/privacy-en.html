Pet Lineup – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026

1. Who we are

Pet Lineup (Chinese title: 萌寵排排站; "the Game") is provided by Luca studio. If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at kpyluca.studio@gmail.com.

2. The short version

  • The Game does not require an account. We do not collect your name, phone number, email address or contacts.
  • Your game progress is stored only on your device. It is not uploaded to us, and we do not run any server that stores player data.
  • The Game does not use your camera, photos, microphone, location or contacts, and never asks for those permissions.
  • The mobile version shows ads. Ads are provided by Google's mobile advertising service, which collects some device and usage data (see Section 4).
  • We do not ask for permission to "track" you. Ads are not targeted using your activity in other companies' apps or websites.

3. Data that stays on your device

  • Game progress: the level you have reached, the board of a level you left unfinished, and whether you have seen the tutorial.
  • Settings: your language, and whether sound effects and music are on.
  • Purchase status: whether you have bought "Remove Ads".

This data is stored only on your device so the Game can work. We cannot see it or access it.

4. Advertising

The mobile version has two kinds of ads:

  • Rewarded ads: shown only when you choose to watch one in exchange for extra undos, revealing hidden tiles, a hint, or an extra holding slot.
  • Between-level ads: shown between two levels, never in the middle of play.

After you buy "Remove Ads", no ads are shown.

Ads are provided by Google LLC's mobile advertising service (Google AdMob). When ads are shown, that service may collect and process:

  • IP address, which is used to estimate your general location (such as country or city)
  • Device information, such as model, operating system version, language and screen size
  • Device identifiers (used for ad frequency counting and fraud prevention)
  • Ad interactions, such as whether an ad was shown, completed or tapped
  • App performance and diagnostic data

This data is used to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and abuse, and improve the advertising service. The Game does not ask for tracking permission, so the advertising service does not receive your advertising identifier, and ads are served in a non-personalized way.

To learn how Google uses this data, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

We never share your game progress with the advertising service.

5. Purchases

"Remove Ads" is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple. We do not receive your card number, payment method or Apple account details. The system only tells the Game whether the purchase has been made on this device. Purchase history and refunds are handled under Apple's rules.

6. Web demo

A demo version of the Game can also be played in a web browser. The web demo has no real ads and no purchases; your progress is stored in your browser. The page is hosted by GitHub's web hosting service, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Deleting the Game also deletes the game progress and settings on your device. This cannot be undone. A purchased "Remove Ads" can be restored with "Restore Purchases".
  • We keep no backup of your data. Game progress does not move automatically when you change phones.
  • Data collected by the advertising service is retained and deleted under Google's policies. We cannot look it up or delete it for you.

8. Children

The Game is not designed specifically for children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Children under 13 (or the age required in your region) should use the Game with a parent's supervision and consent.

9. Your rights

Under the Personal Data Protection Act of the Republic of China (Taiwan) and the laws of your region, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to object to certain processing. Because we do not store your personal data on any server, we usually have nothing to provide. For ad-related data, please contact Google. You are welcome to email us with any questions.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date. Significant changes will be announced in the Game.

If the Chinese and English versions differ, the Chinese version prevails.
